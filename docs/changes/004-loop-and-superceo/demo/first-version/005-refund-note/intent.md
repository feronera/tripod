# Intent: Refund note on cancelled orders

Status: draft

## Problem
Customers whose order is cancelled ask support whether they will be refunded. 6 of 40 tickets last week.

## Users
Customers, on the order page.

## Success measure
The order page shows a refund note on cancelled orders within this change; refund questions in tickets drop from 6 to 2 per week within a month.

## Risk
Risk: high

1. Law or regulation: yes. A refund statement is a promise under consumer law.
2. Seen directly by customers: yes.
3. Rollback within minutes: yes.
4. Personal or sensitive data: no.

## Constraints
- The wording must be approved by legal before release.

## Open questions
- None.
